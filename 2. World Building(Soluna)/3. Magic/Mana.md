Every person is born with a certain amount of mana that their bodies possess. It is now akin to their blood. Important thing to note is that mana, or the concept of magic did not exist until 212 years ago. But slowly, over the years it became an inseparable part of the world. It is as abundant as the air itself.

"Envision your body as if it was a cup. You can pour a finite amount of liquid into it. There are many types of cups out there, with different sizes and shapes, used for all sorts of different beverages. That is how mana works, some have a naturally high capacity, while others can only hold a certain amount of mana. While this innately decided at one's birth, through training and conditioning you can change or improve your constitution, however the results vary and seldom allow full mastery of a different mana type or increasing your capacity by more than half." - A professor giving a lecture at the [[Astral Sanctuary|Astral Sanctuary]].

"Now you may wonder, what if a person doesn't have a cup, or there is a crack in it. Well, my dear students, those cases are few, but they are grim."

"If one were to be born without a mana vessel, or in the case of some of our seniors, old enough to have been born before the surge of mana in our atmosphere, they won't be at a risk of death, however they might find day to day life difficult. While we can't tell the difference, to them it's like the air is covered in thick, heavy fog which makes even moving and breathing difficult. They won't pass from this but they will remain handicapped for the rest of their lives."

"For those whose mana vessels are damaged, the prognosis looks much worse. As you all know, mana to us has become like blood. If one were to run out, it will spell certain death for them. Now don't be alarmed, the mana we use to cast spells can run out freely, that is something we can regenerate given enough time, the problem lies if the mana that circulates in one's system, their blood, begins to leak or dwindle. If it runs out completely, the person will unfortunately wither and pass away, leaving nothing behind but a pile of ash. Yes, I am referring to the [[Ashen Blood|Ashen Blood]]."

"Yes, we are searching for cures for both of these conditions, but we have had no luck so far. But perhaps someone amongst you will bring about the change in the future. I am counting on you. Don't give up."
