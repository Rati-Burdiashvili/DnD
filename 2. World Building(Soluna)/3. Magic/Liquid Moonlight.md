Liquid Moonlight is a viscous, transparent silver liquid that evaporates in sunlight. This is what the [[Moonlit Beasts|Moonlit Beasts]] are born from. As the moonlight descends and spreads on the planet, these beasts emerge from it.

The Liquid Moonlight is a highly volatile substance. Every trace of it evaporates at the lightest touch of sunlight. It is not affected by artificial light. It can be kept in vials made of special material that will not let light through.

The liquid remains dormant besides weeping nights, during which it begins to "act violently". It tries to escape containment and reach the larger pools of moonlight. It might be safe to assume that [[Moonlit Beasts|Moonlit Beasts]] cannot form, if there's not enough space or enough of the substance.

The possible applications of moonlight are still under research. Direct contact with the skin is not advised. It drains [[Mana]] from any living being it comes in contact with, and prolonged contact will result in [[Mana]] Sickness and eventual death.

"The substance is densely packed with [[Mana|Mana]], able to hold multitudes more than anything or anyone we have seen so far. If we can find a way to safely extract it... we will be able to change the world..." a researcher at [[Prometheus Institute|The Prometheus Institute]].
