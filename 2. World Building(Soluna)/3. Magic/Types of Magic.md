There exist 3 distinct types of magic:

#### **Arcane**
This magic type is most common to Wizards, Artificers and Bards.

Arcane magic is cultivated by its users for years. They act as a filter for the surrounding [[Mana|mana]], channeling it through their bodies to converting it to their will. While Arcane spellcasters have their own naturally high reserves of [[Mana|mana]], their specialty lies in utilizing the surroundings to boost their capabilities.

Arcane spellcasters spend years of hard work and dedication to attain their perfect vision of magic, which makes them extremely versatile.


#### **Primal**
This magic type is most common to Sorcerers, Druids and Rangers

Primal casters are born with an extremely high amount of [[Mana|mana]] reserves. Unlike Arcane spellcasters, they don't need to rely on their surroundings to channel the magic, everything comes from within their bodies. However such high natural talent and power usually means their magic is less flexible.


#### **Primordial**
This magic type is most common to Clerics, Paladins and Warlocks.

Primordial mages draw their magic from beyond their bodies and the surroundings. Through a strong faith, devotion or even a contract, their bodies become vessels, hosts for primordial [[Mana|mana]] to take root. Their magical talents can be seen as a combination of Primal and Arcane.

They tend to have a higher capacity for [[Mana|mana]] compared to Arcanists, but lower than primalists, while needing shape the [[Mana|mana]] into their will within their bodies first(primal) and then channel it to the outside(Arcane).

While Arcane and Primal magic can be cultivated through training and growth, Primordial's growth is deeply tied to the moment the [[Mana|mana]] nested itself in the person's body.
