These crystals, as the name suggests are a concentrated form of magic. Despite their usually small size, they can hold quite a bit of [[Mana|mana]] in them, enough to power average appliances for months on end.

#### **Size**

1. Small - majority of crystals are of this size. They are used for everyday items are the main source of power generation.
2. Medium - this size is still rather common. It is used for heavier machinery for more power demand, and handheld weapons.
3. Large - a rather rare sized crystal. They hold 5x times as much [[Mana|mana]] in them as a medium crystal would. They are mostly used in specialized equipment and canons.
4. Unique - huge crystals can only be harvested from moonlit shepherds. They take on unique properties and are either unused due to their volatile nature or are only utilized with specific equipment designed with the crystals in mind.
