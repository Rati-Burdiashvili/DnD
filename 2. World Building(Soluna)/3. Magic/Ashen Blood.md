Once a person's vessel begins to leak the [[Mana|mana]] that keeps them alive, their fate becomes sealed. They have been stricken with Ashen Blood. There is no cure, only a countdown.

The affliction progress in multiple stages:

#### **Stage 1**
General weakness, loss of appetite, occasional nausea, vomiting, muscle fatigue.

#### **Stage 2**
Degradation of senses, chronic fatigue, difficulty breathing, ash-like flakes begin to develop on the arms and legs.

#### **Stage 3**
During this stage, symptoms begin to wary between the afflicted. The only common symptom is the ashy growths on their skin begins to spread from the limbs to their torso. 

Some patients have lost the ability to spell cast, while others reported an even higher affinity for it. Some experienced hallucinations, some became fully paralyzed and so on. It is unique to every afflicted.

#### **Stage 4**
Death. Once the ash on the skin has progressed enough throughout the body, and reached the heart, death becomes imminent. At this stage, the afflicted have not even a single day left to live. The only blessing is that during their last day, the symptoms that had been plaguing their lives disappear, but it is only a small blessing, for there is no salvation.

There is no cure for the Ashen Blood. There are no methods to suppress it either. The only thing the afflicted can do to prolong their lives is to cease any and all spellcasting, and take care of their bodies.
