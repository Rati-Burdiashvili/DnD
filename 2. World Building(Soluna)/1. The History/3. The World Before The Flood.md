+ 0000
+ The Rise
+ The modern calendar was created to signify a new era in the history of Soluna, the new rule of the Maregel Empire. Not many historical records have survived to the modern age, but from what has been preserved, it is known that the calendar was based on the coronation of a new emperor, Kasdiel. Not much is known about history prior to this. According to surviving records and folktales, Kasdiel was a benevolent human king. Unlike his predacessors, Kasdiel focused on the advancment of his kingdom and his people. Under his rule, The Empire prospered, and some historians claim that it technologically advanced by several centuries. He accepted any and all people under his wing, as long as they had something to offer for the Empire. This helped unite the different species who had been at odds for centuries, allowing them to prosper in a new era of peace, much to the dismay of multiple high ranking members of the empire's nobility. The emperor was never without protection, but unfortunately one of the assassinations attempts succeeded, murdering Kasdiel in cold blood and leaving the empire without an heir.

The races: Human, Elf, Dwarves, Halflings, Assimar, Orc, Gnome

+ 2nd century
+ The Fall
+ Peace was sustained for mere hundred years within the empire, but tensions between the different species kept rising, as if someone was sowing seeds of doubt amongst the people. As the nobility kept fighting each other both in broad daylight and in the shadows, to seize as much power as they could, the empire fractured into many different segments, each with their own ruler, agenda and a thirst for power.
  
+ 2nd century
+ The War of Species
+ What followed was a brutal war, the likes of which the world had never seen before. Countless young soldiers, a lot of whom were sent to the battlefield against their will, were forced to fight and murder each other in cold blood. Far, far too much blood was shed. After a year of this, the 4 surviving territories, who had absorbed the rest of the empire's remanants, signed a truth between each other, "ending" the war, but nontheless the tensions remained high for many years to come.

+ 3rd century
+ Revolution
+ The details of what exactly happened are unclear, but we know that one of the territories' people revolted against their supposed rulers. Replacing those in power, who had been tormenting the citizens. A new power was born, one that the other 3 territories did not take kindly to. They planed to wage war once more, driven by greed and a lust for power, but soon found themselves abandoned. Their own people had abandoned them, becoming refugees for the new territory. Eventually, the 3 territories now left powerless, became absorbed by a new power, now named the Free Republic of Orlon, essentially unfying the continent. 

+ 4-10th century
+ The Era of Peace
+ It was the first time in Soluna's history to have maintained peace for such a long time. The people reclaimed their abandoned lands, reclaimed their homes and buried their loved ones. During these 600 years, the world advanced at a pace never seen before. New law and order was put in place. Education became widely accessible, technology advanced by leaps and bounds. Quality of life improved and the people were content.

+ 11th century
+ New Continent
+ After centuries, something unprecedented and thought to have been impossible happened. A new continent was discovered. One much different from Orlon(they needed a name for the continent, now that there were two, so it was named after the republic). Sailors on an expedition from Orlon crashed ashore on the new continent during a storm, finding themselves in the care of species they had never seen before, that were nothing like them. Through much effort, communication became possible. The sailors spent a few years there, learning and preparing for the journey home. Some decided to stay, while some of the people of Karne decided to join them on their way back to Orlon.
  
The races: Dragonborn, Tiefling, Aarakocra, Bugbear, Kobold, Centaur, Genasi

+ The Karne Continent
+ Its history
+ The Karne continent did not keep a calendar system like Orlon. They only counted the moons and the seasons, letting the years pass them by. Unlike Orlon, the people of Karne were not self governing. Their continent was home to mystical creatures known as Dragons. They bestowed upon the people knowledge and granted them safety, in exchange for offerings and worship. They lived in coexistence for what some theorize may have been millenia, but after Karne's contact with Orlon, the balance shifted.
  
+ The Karne Continet
+ The Departure
+ With the sharing of cultures and technology that came with the unity of the two continents, the dragons made the decision to make their exit. One day, around 50 years after the established contact, the dragons were nowhere to be found. The reason for their departure is unclear, however there were two main theories. 
  
One side believed the Dragons, who essentially served as the protectors of their worshippers, deemed that the people no longer needed their protection and decided to watch over them from afar.

The other side believed the Dragons grew dissatisfied with the distance that was created between them and their worshippers. As people came to rely on them less, they lost interest and left them behind.

The real reason mis unclear and we may never know the truth, but the present remained the same. The two continents were now "unified" and the people had none to rely on, except themselves.
