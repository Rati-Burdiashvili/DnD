Current year is 314 AF. The population has grown from 3000 survivors to 10000 and the growth has now stabilized. It took a massive effort to reach this far.

The best minds that managed to gather here devised a desperate strategy that made them lead a difficult life, in an already hellish world. In the history that has been successfully kept, we know of mankind's slow and gradual expansion, as they slowly managed to claim and colonize the remaining land. They planted seeds for the future generations to hopefully sprout.

We know of six figures, now referred to as the Founding Six. Each one founded and spearheaded a different movement. Some are still alive to this day. They are as follows:
- Orlaith, The Seer of Stars, The Founder of the [[Astral Sanctuary|Astral Sanctuary]] 
	- Status: Alive
- Yulia, The Devout, The Archbishop of the Church
	- Status: Alive
- The Baron, The Strategist behind [[Umbra]]
	- Status: Passed from natural causes
- Mako, The Surgeon of Hope, The Founder of The [[Lavender Clinic]]
	- Status: Passed from natural causes
- Sirius, The Inventor, The Founder of The [[Prometheus Institute]]
	- Status: Passed from natural causes
- Wilder, The Protector, The Seeker of [[Elysium]]
	- Status: MIA
