It has been 300 years since the flood.

Soluna's night sky was once blessed with twin silver jewels.

One night, same as any other. People on Soluna witnessed one of their moons get dusted by a violet mist and soon after the moon began to melt. Pouring [[98. Glossary/Liquid Moonlight|liquid moonlight]] onto the surface of Soluna. Birthed from the liquid were creatures never seen by any. Taking shape of abominations, grotesque and eldritch, never the same but always coated in the same moonlight silver. The ever orbiting [[Weeping Moon|weeping moon]] coated the lands and sea, the [[Moonlit Beasts]] reaped havoc as they marched the whole globe the entire weeping night. Many lives they took, not even leaving a drop of begin behind. Salvation came for when the first rays on sunlight hit the lands, melting away the moonlight - leaving no trace behind.

For a while, despite this horrific disaster, mankind thought they were safe, but not a week later, the Second Moon shattered. The sound, akin to glass breaking, spread throughout the world, and as everyone looked up, all they saw were the pieces of the [[98. Glossary/Shattered Moon|Shattered Moon]] hurling down. The pieces of the [[98. Glossary/Shattered Moon|Shattered Moon]] destroyed countries, killed millions and left devastation in their wake.

Mankind was left with picking up the pieces of their once great civilization. But the destruction of their two moons were not without consequences. The tides became dramatically weaker. As a result, the ecology of the biosphere crumbled. Over the course of several years, many oceanic species, both mammals and fish, died out. Oxygen in the water stopped renewing, killing the plants as well. The land animals that fed on the oceanic creatures also died out in starvation. And the oceans became more and more polluted as the tides ceased to shift. The world entered a slow and deadly stagnation.

If that was all, perhaps mankind may have bounced back, but that was not the end. It was only the beginning.

To this day, the moon continues to weep, once every 28 days, as if mourning that it will never be full again. The tragedy repeats itself again and again, with no end in sight... but the consequences of this are for a different discussion.

Over the course of 10 years, civilizations fell apart. Billions perished and the world lost all hope of ever going back to normal. The water level kept rising rapidly, so fast in fact, that it was noticeable on a daily basis. However many survivors were left, among them some of the best minds of mankind, scrambled together and took whatever they could salvage and fled to the only place that could offer them more time, the Coronal Mountain Range. The tallest mountain range in the world, with 12 mountains situated in a perfect circle.

Survival was difficult, the conditions were harsh, but as the weather got warmer each year, it became easier, and the [[Moonlit Beasts]] had a harder time reaching here. In 10 years, the water finally stopped rising, just before it swallowed and drowned the last hope of mankind.
