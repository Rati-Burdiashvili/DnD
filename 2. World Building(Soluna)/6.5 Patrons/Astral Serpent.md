## **Astral Serpent** 

The Astral Serpent has no voice. None of its actions will ever utter a sound. 

---

In the silent expanse of the Astral Sea, where stars are born and die, the Serpent of Preservation weaves its cosmic tapestry. Its eyes, ancient and fathomless, watch over its home, ever vigilant for those who would disrupt its existence. 

With each celestial breath, the Serpent's scales shimmer with the cosmic light, a silent testament to its eternal vigil. Its purpose, as old as the stars themselves, is simple: to preserve. And no matter the cost, it will continue to do so until the last star fades from the cosmic sky. 

---

#### **Nature**
Apathetic, Dormant, Silent, Impartial, Devoted, Clear, Dragon 

#### **Ideals** 

*Preservation and Survival.* The Serpent lets out a silent roar as a testament to its purpose. 

*Cut off all that is harmful.* Sever the wound before it can fester.
