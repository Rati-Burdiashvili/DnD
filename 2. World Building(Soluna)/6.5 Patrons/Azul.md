
## **Azul**

"Everything and Everyone flows within the confines of Causality.  
It flows within the shape of a spiral. 
It's easy to get swept up in its raging currents. 
Resisting it is futile, follow the flow and bide your time. 
It will come for you, no matter your desire. 
You will only have one chance."  

---

Azul is a man full of mysteries. He never gives more than necessary and sometimes even less than that. His demeanor does not reveal much to you either, despite how direct he can be about "purpose." All will be revealed in due time, or so he says. Yet he never breaks his promise. 

---

#### **Nature** 

Empathetic, Proactive, Conversational, Mysterious, Devoted, Seeker, Isolated, Human(?) 

#### **Ideals** 

*Stand The Test of Time.* Eventually everything will have to take this test. Be prepared. 

*Bear The Gravity of It All.* Without conviction, without purpose, can you really bear the weight of it?
