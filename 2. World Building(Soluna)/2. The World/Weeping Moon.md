The Weeping Moon is still a horrifying sight to behold in the night sky. It looms over the planet, as if threatening to melt and drown the planet any moment now.

Even with the naked eye, it can clearly be seen that its surface keeps moving around, like a liquid, as its holes keep changing size, depth and location.
