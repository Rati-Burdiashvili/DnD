DISCLAIMER: DETAILS ON THE SHEPHERDS WILL BE ADDED

Moonlit Shepherds are a unique, significantly deadlier versions of [[98. Glossary/Moonlit Beasts|Moonlit beasts]]. In the last 300 years there have been only 6 sightings of them.

They all displayed unique properties and shared next to no resemblance to each other. The reason they have been called shepherds is because they gather and guide the [[98. Glossary/Moonlit Beasts|moonlit beasts]], whether it is consciously or subconsciously. The beasts gather around the shepherd and heed every one of its commands.

This turns the Weeping Nights that much deadlier. The beasts are dangerous enough to handle as a horde, but once that horde has a leader, it turns into an army, not to mention the strength of the leader.
