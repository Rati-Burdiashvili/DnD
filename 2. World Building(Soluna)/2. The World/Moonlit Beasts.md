It is impossible to describe the beasts. They are far too varied in their appearance. Each one takes on a different shape and form. Some even claim that the beasts take on a different form for each individual.

The beasts mindlessly migrate together to the closest signs of life. It is unclear how they choose the direction to migrate towards but it is speculated that they can sense life or [[Mana|mana]](at least according to limited observations during the weeping nights).

The beasts take on the characteristics of the forms they are born with, such as canines acting beast like, humanoids wielding weapons, avians taking flight and so on.

The beasts attempt to maul any living creature in sight and then absorb the [[Mana|mana]] within them. As a result, the beasts usually grow in size and power, becoming even more dangerous.

Occasionally, there is a special entity among the Moonlight Beasts. Each time one has appeared, it has resulted in drastic casualties. There are no similarities between the special beasts, except for the fact that each one seems to possesses or at least mimics intelligence. They lead and direct the horde of beasts to cause devastation.
