### **Overview of Education**

![[astral sanctuary.jpg]]

The Astral Sanctuary was established as a necessity to study the newly discovered source of power, magic. It has changed and grown over the years, but it's purpose has remained the same, study [[Mana|mana]], magic and learn how to harness its power.

Once every 10 years, the Astral sanctuary conducts a screening test across all newborn children. They test for their aptitude in magic and their capacity to wield [[Mana|mana]]. The test isn't dangerous by any means. The children simply need to step into a magic circle that is a mix of detect magic and identify, and it gives information on their aptitude. The only reason it's just new children that are tested, is because everyone older has already been tested.

The children that have potential to become mages receive an evaluation first, especially in cases where the amount of [[Mana|mana]] or potential they have is far too high and may become dangerous if they don't learn to control it. Otherwise, the children and their families are given support and guidance, until the budding mage is old enough to move into the Astral Sanctuary for their studies.

There are currently 6 tiers for students. The students may take a written and practical test to advance to next tier once every six months. Each tier teaches more and more advanced theory and practical application of magic, however you can already be considered graduated upon completion of the third tier, the rest is if you wish to climb to greater heights still within the sanctuary.

Other than tiers, the sanctuary divides students by schools of magic(evocation, transmutation, etc.) based on their aptitude.


### **Overview of Research**

The Astral Sanctuary is not only an academy for budding mages, it is also a center of research. Day and night the mages toil away to refine and create new spells, trying to break the barrier of what is possible.
