### **Overview**

As the name suggests, it's as simple as that. A clinic.

With the population decreased to such a staggeringly low number, every life became even more precious than it already was. That was the core belief behind the swift founding of this clinic. The lavender clinic mixes old school methods of treatment, including surgery, with magic to achieve the best results.

Mako, the only surviving doctor during the flood era, took on many apprentices to pass down as much of her knowledge and skills as she possibly good. She was an amazing doctor and it is safe to say, that without her teachings, there would have been no future for many, many people.
