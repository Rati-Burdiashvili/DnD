#### **Stat Arrays** 

When filling out your ability scores, you can choose one of the following stat arrays. 

- The Specialist. 17 / 12 / 12 / 11 / 10 / 10 
    
- The Mad Man. 15 / 15 / 14 / 11 / 11 / 10  
    
- The Razzle Dazzle. 13 / 13 / 13 / 13 / 13 / 13 
    
- The Madder Man. 16 / 16 / 13 / 10 / 10 / 8  
    
- The Behemoth. 18 / 16 / 14 / 9 / 9 / 7 
    

#### **ASI Versatility** 

When a character reaches a level when they would gain an ASI, you gain both an ASI and a feat. However all feats that would give a +1 to a Stat, lose that +1.

#### **Character Options** 

You can use any WOTC-published sourcebook to create your character. If you want to use any UA or Lolzies/Sky homebrew, make sure to bring it up to the DM beforehand. Any racial restrictions for subclasses have been lifted.

#### **Human Clause** 

Standard and Variant Human cannot be used. Instead, use Human (Schmariant) in the Races section. 

#### **Racial Bonuses** 

All races now start with a free feat of their choosing. The Ability Score Increase features in the Races section only represent a suggestion of what to expect that race to have. We use Lineage rules for all ASI gained from Races and Subraces. Choose one of: (a) Choose any +2; choose any other +1 (b) Choose three different +1. 

Races that gain a total of +4 to ability scores instead choose one from above and a +1 to any stat unchosen. 

#### **Backgrounds** 

You are free to create whatever background you feel is most appropriate. In doing so, you gain proficiency in 2 skills of your choosing,  and either 2 languages or 2 tool proficiencies or 1 of each. You also have 25 gp or the world equivalent. Backgrounds that grant a feat (e. g: Strixhaven Backgrounds) can only be taken if you take their corresponding feat using your free starting feat.
