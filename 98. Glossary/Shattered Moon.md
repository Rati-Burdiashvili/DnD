The shattering of the moon still remains a mystery. There are no known records of what exactly happened on the moon to cause it to shatter(eyewitnesses). Every so often, debris from the moon hurl towards the earth to this day.

The Shattered Moon is silver in color with a shade of red striking across its surface. There is a ring of debris that has formed around that keeps circling it.
